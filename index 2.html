THE
HOLLOW

A MOBILE FIRST-PERSON NIGHTMARE

You wake beneath a dead forest. Three relic shards are hidden in the ruins. Something hears every step you take.

HOW TO WIN

1. Explore the forest and collect all 3 glowing blue relic shards.
2. Avoid the monsters. If one gets too close, run away!
3. Once you have all 3 shards, follow the corridors to the gate near the starting side of the map and reach it to escape.

MOVE: hold the left joystick
LOOK: drag the right side
RUN: hold RUN · USE: tap near objects
